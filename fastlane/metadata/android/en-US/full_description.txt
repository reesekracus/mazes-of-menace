Mazes of Menace is NetHack 5.0 for your phone. The game is the real, unmodified NetHack: the same dungeon, monsters, items, rules and endings. Only the interface is new, and it is made for touch. You never need a keyboard.

Descend through the Dungeons of Doom, past the Gnomish Mines, Sokoban and the Oracle, down into Gehennom, to recover the Amulet of Yendor for your god.

Built for touch:
- A d-pad for moving (hold an arrow to run) and a row of action buttons you can customize
- Tap the map to travel, long-press to look, drag to pan, pinch to zoom
- Item questions open your inventory as a list with pictures; no item letters to remember
- Buttons appear for whatever you are standing on: stairs, fountains, altars, items
- Every command is in a searchable list, by name
- Game text that mentions keys is rewritten to name the buttons instead

Also included:
- DawnLike pixel-art tiles (or NetHack's own tiles, or classic ASCII)
- Phone and tablet layouts, upright or sideways, with a left-handed option
- Auto-save when you leave the app, plus backup and restore of your saves
- The full end-of-game summary, and a list of your past games
- Vibration and a red warning when your hit points get low
- Built-in help pages, and NetHack 5.0's own tutorial
